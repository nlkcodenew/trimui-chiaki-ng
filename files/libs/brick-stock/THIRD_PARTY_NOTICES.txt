Brick Pro Stock OS compatibility runtime
========================================

These unmodified AArch64 shared libraries come from the TrimUI TG5050 SDK
sysroot. They are fallback dependencies only when the device model is sun50iw10;
Stock OS libraries keep priority. Other devices, including sun55iw3/Spruce OS,
continue to use only their system libraries.
